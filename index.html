CrabsMcChaffey ENT Deck

With nearly 9,000 downloads, the CrabsMcChaffey ENT Deck has become a widely used Anki resource for medical students and otolaryngology residents. It is built for high-yield, longitudinal review of core ENT knowledge through spaced repetition.
